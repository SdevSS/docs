### Step 1: Ads System Setup 

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="gear" title="Creating Ads">
    <img height="50" src="/images/tutorials/dashboard_ads_setup.jpg"/>
    Access the [Ads Page](https://central.azguard.my.id/ads/) and click on **Setup** on of the slots.

    #### Setup Steps:
    1. Select the designated **Target Folder** that users will be whitelisted after ads
    2. Select one of these 3 providers: **Linkvertise, Lootlabs, or Work.Ink**
    3. Determine how many **Ads Needed** to get whitelisted (1 - 10)
    4. Set the **Duration** of the key in hours 
    5. Copy the **Destination Link** (it's optional for **Lootlabs**)

    <Danger> KEEP THIS TAB OPEN, CONTINUE TO THE NEXT STEP </Danger>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 2: Ads Provider Setup

<Tips> If you are confused, join our [Discord Server](https://discord.azguard.my.id) to get official support / others help </Tips>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="paperclip" title="Linkvertise Setup">
    Register / Log-in to your [Linkvertise Account](https://linkvertise.com) first.

    #### Provider Steps:
    1. Access the [Linkvertise Dashboard](https://publisher.linkvertise.com/dashboard#dashboard) from your account.
    2. Click on **Post & Earn** button on the bottom of the page.
    3. Choose type **Link**, enter the **Destination Link** you copied from the step before.
    4. Enter other details such as **Visibility, Title, and Ads Action** up to you.
    5. Click on **Publish** and copy the **Linkvertise Link** such as ``https://link-center.net/1329771/8DPY6F0ody7A``
    6. Go to **Settings Tab**, enable **Anti - Bypassing**, copy your token, and **Save Settings**.

    
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="paperclip" title="Lootlabs Setup">
    Register / Log-in to your [Lootlabs Account](https://lootlabs.gg/sign-up) first.

    #### Provider Steps:
    1. Access the [Lootlabs Dashboard](https://creators.lootlabs.gg/dashboard) from your account.
    2. Click on **Create Single Monetized Links** button on the top-left of the page.
    3. Enter any name for your Lootlabs Link, it wont matter.
    4. Enter any links on **Destination URL** such as ``youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ``
    5. Enter other details such as **Theme, Thumbnail, Task, Revenue, etc** up to you.
    5. Click on **Create Link** and copy the **Lootlabs Link** such as ``https://loot-link.com/s?tOcieUIJ``
    6. Go to **Advanced Tab** on top, **Generate & Copy** the API Key.
    

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="paperclip" title="Work.Ink Setup">
    Register / Log-in to your [Work.Ink Account](https://auth.work.ink/) first.

    #### Provider Steps:
    1. Access the [Work.Ink Dashboard](https://dashboard.work.ink/) from your account.
    2. Click on **Create Link** on the top-left of your side.
    3. Enter any title for your Work.Ink Link, it wont matter.
    4. Enter the **Destination Link** you copied from the step before.
    5. Enter other details such as **Custom URL, Description, or Youtube** if needed.
    5. Click on **Shorten Link** and copy the **Work.Ink Link** such as ``https://work.ink/1YNM/sigmaboy``
    
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 3: Publishing Ads

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="rocket" title="Publishing Ads">
    <img height="50" src="/images/tutorials/dashboard_ads_setup.jpg"/>
    Go back to the previous [Ads Tab](https://central.azguard.my.id/ads/).

    #### Setup Steps:
    1. Paste the **API Key** from the previous step (except for Work.Ink)
    2. Enter your **Provider Link** from the previous step.
    3. Click on **Save** and you will get the ads URL such as: <br />
    ``https://azguard.my.id/ads/41uKe7LEK5ltk2n``
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Congratulations, **Ads Setup** is done, you can now distribute that link to your users.
