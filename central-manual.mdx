### Step 2: Manage Scripts & Folders
<Tip> Script Folders acts a mandatory grouping for Scripts to use other features </Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="folder" title="Create a Folder">
    <img height="50" src="/images/tutorials/dashboard_folder.jpg"/>
    #### Creating Steps:
    1. Kindly, click on **+** on your left side of the [Scripts Page](https://central.azguard.my.id/scripts/)
    2. Enter some details about the folder*

    _*You may use COMPANY_FOLDER format for name (ex: AZGUARD_PREMIUM)_
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="file-code" title="Upload a Script">
    <img height="50" src="/images/tutorials/dashboard_scripts_new.jpg"/>
    #### Creating Steps:
    1. Kindly, click on **Create New** on your right side of the [Scripts Page](https://central.azguard.my.id/scripts/)
    2. Enter the name for your script
    3. Enter the initial version **_(It's a string)_**
    4. Assign it to a folder that you just made
    5. Upload your script in a [Lua (5.1 is better)](https://www.lua.org/) format
    6. Decide if this script is keyless **_(you can change it later)_**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="pen" title="Editing Scripts / Folders">
    <img height="50" src="/images/tutorials/dashboard_scripts_edit.jpg"/>
    Your central should now be somewhat like this. Here are some things you can do:

    #### Folder Actions:
    - You may Edit your Folder **HWID Reset Allowance & Name**. _NO DELETING!_

    #### Script Actions:
    1. You may see **Scripts Name, Folder, Version, and Loader**.
    2. If you do want to change it, click on **Configure**
    3. If you want to delete it, click on **Delete** (NO UNDO)

  </Accordion>  
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 3: Manage Keys

<Note> Remember, ``-1`` as a duration always meant **LIFETIME** </Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="folder" title="Select a Folder">
    <img height="50" src="/images/tutorials/dashboard_keys_select.jpg"/>
    Access the [Keys Page](https://central.azguard.my.id/keys/) on you AzarineGuard Central

    #### Page Functions:
    1. You can see **Number of Scripts** and **Total Keys** contained in that folder
    2. You may download all keys in TXT by clickinng the **Download Icon**
    3. Click on **Configure** to manage all keys in that Folder
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="key" title="Manage Keys from Folder">
    <img height="50" src="/images/tutorials/dashboard_keys_manage.jpg"/>

    #### Page Functions:
    1. You can see **Key, Status, Banned, Expiry, HWID** in the explorer
    2. You may search for **Keys** on the top-right search bar
    3. You may change entries shown from 10 - 100 per page
    4. You may downlod all keys by clicking the **Download** button
    5. You may generate bulk keys by clicking the **Generate** button
    6. You may import keys in JSON by clicking **Import** button (good for migrations)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
